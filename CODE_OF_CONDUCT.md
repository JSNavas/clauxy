# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith.

## What that means here

Welcome: direct technical disagreement, blunt review, saying "this is wrong and
here is why", admitting you do not know something.

Not welcome: personal attacks, dismissing someone rather than their argument,
harassment of any kind, sexualised language or imagery, publishing anyone's
private information.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the
project is represented.

## Enforcement

Report problems through a
[private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) or by contacting the
maintainers. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions, and may block accounts, when this document is not met.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
shortened deliberately.
